Orbis · concept version 1

The planet fights back

You land on a planet that is alive, and it treats you as an infection. You build a fortified settlement while it answers with swarms, giant creatures and plants, storms and deadly nights. You spread across the globe one region at a time and choose what to study: the exotic material, to build a war machine; the wildlife, to bond with the planet; or the ruins of the lost civilisation, to take what they left. Then the rival lands.

The concept on one page

The playerOne deep side: a corporate-funded military expedition with colonists
The enemyThe planet itself: alive, its swarms, giants, plants, storms and nights are its immune system
Why we're thereThe company's prize, the colonists' way out, and the truth in the ruins
The prizeOne substance, four faces: core energy, an exotic material, the life, the ancients' tech
The ruleRicher ground, angrier planet; one agitation meter drives it
The globeOne persistent planet, region by region; land at the equator, the poles are the endgame
The baseWalls, turrets, power grid, territory, adjacency, a settlement; crawler outposts; costly terraforming; earned orbital support
EconomyMass and energy as a flow, plus people
CommandNo heroes: formations, stances, control groups, rally and patrol
TechA shared trunk, then material, wildlife and ancients branches; mixing limited, the top locks
EndingsDominate, Stabilise (bond, then defend the planet from your own company), Inherit
TonePumped and grim, then split by branch, with the frontier dream under it
First sliceSurvive the swarm: flat test map, walls, turrets, power, flow, troops, ten waves
LookRealistic and gritty
NameOrbis; the substance is named later

Orbis · the game concept

The planet fights back

A planet-scale RTS for a builder and a turtle. You land on a living planet that treats you as an infection, hold a fortified settlement against its swarms, giants, storms and nights, and choose what to study: the material, the wildlife or the ruins. Each leads to its own ending.

Concept models · 3D
Orbis concept models Code-made 3D models on turntables: the colonist, the ancients, the Given running and standing, the Eldest, the swarm beast and brute, the guardian, dropship and turret, spore towers, the creeping forest, a crystal seam, a dome and a pit, plus everyone side by side to scale.
Lore round 1 · open
The Given The ancients as colonists before you, who fed their own to the planet and were swallowed: the story in five ages, the forks, two lineages (the planet's own life and the Given) for two branches, the pits down to the heart, the look, how it plays, the endings, agitation by region, and the questions.
Weapons round 1 · open
What you fight with Nine weapon families: projectiles, explosives, fire, lightning, lasers, plasma, the substance's own, natural and the ancients' tech. What each beats, what it costs (mass, power, noise), where it sits in the tech tree, how the world affects it, and the questions.
Settlement round 1 · open
Digging in and living there The mining ladder (surface extractors to deep drills), machines that reshape the land, where your people live (domes, a growing city, or domes that grow towns), how it changes with your path, and the questions.
Environment round 1 · open
A world that isn't Earth What films and games do to make a world feel alien, the biomes from equator to pole with the substance rising toward the poles, swamps, goo seas and glass rivers instead of oceans, plants and storms by biome, the sky (moons, a ring, close planets), the desert giant, what each region is good for, what Orbis can draw today, and the questions.
